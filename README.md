# Pharma Sales & Commercial Intelligence Hub

An end-to-end portfolio project built for a **Sales & Data Analyst** role in the pharmaceutical industry.

## Business Scope

The project analyses:

- Sales performance by representative, product, customer and territory
- Monthly sales trends and gross profit
- Promotional campaign performance and ROI
- Quotation and tender status, conversion and follow-up
- Sales database quality and integrity
- Product demand and business opportunity indicators

## Tools Demonstrated

Microsoft Excel, Power Query concepts, Pivot-ready tables, XLOOKUP-ready master data,
SQL, Python, data cleaning, data validation, Power BI modelling and business reporting.

## Portfolio Description

Developed an end-to-end pharmaceutical sales and commercial intelligence system
using Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI-ready datasets. The solution monitors sales
performance across representatives, products, customers and territories, evaluates
campaign effectiveness, tracks quotation and tender conversion, and surfaces overdue
follow-up actions for the Sales team.

## Important Note

All records are synthetic and created for portfolio and learning purposes. They do not
represent real patient, customer or company information.
